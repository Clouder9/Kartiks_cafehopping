Cup by Cup

One person's honest, occasionally over-caffeinated reviews of cafes worth visiting — and a few worth skipping.

All Espresso Pour Over Work-Friendly Pastries

Marigold Coffee

Fillmore District
☕☕☕☕☕

Bright, citrusy single-origin pour over and genuinely comfortable chairs — rare combination. Gets loud after 10am so come early if you want to actually hear yourself think.

Order this: Kenyan pour over
Visited July 2026

Blackwood & Co.

Downtown
☕☕☕☕☕

Beautiful space, moody lighting, great for a first date. The espresso is fine but not the reason to go. Come for the vibe, not the coffee.

Order this: Oat milk cortado
Visited June 2026

The Daily Grind

Riverside
☕☕☕☕☕

My actual favorite. Small, family-run, the owner remembers your order after two visits. Their cardamom bun alone is worth the drive across town.

Order this: Cortado + cardamom bun
Visited August 2026

Northline Roasters

Old Mill Road
☕☕☕☕☕

Great beans, questionable execution. My latte was scalded twice in a row. Buying their bagged beans to brew at home instead of ordering in-cafe.

Order this: Bag of beans, to go
Visited May 2026

Fern & Filter

Green Valley
☕☕☕☕☕

Plant-filled, quiet, plenty of outlets — my go-to for working remotely for a few hours. Wifi is solid and nobody rushes you off a table.

Order this: Iced oat latte
Visited April 2026

Steep House

Harbor District
☕☕☕☕☕

Mostly a tea house that happens to serve coffee, and it shows. Worth a visit for the matcha, less so for espresso drinks.

Order this: Hojicha latte
Visited March 2026
