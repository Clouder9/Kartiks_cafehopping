Kartik's Cafe Hopping

An internet stranger's honest reviews of work-friendly cafes in Pune, India.

Read first: 1. I compare pricing based on the cost of a simple Cappuccino. 2. This list is mainly for people who like to visit cafes to work on their laptop, read books, doodle on their iPads. Basically for those whole want some peaceful time either by themselves or with a one other person. 3. I am not reviewing coffee here. I'm no coffee expert.

Cafe PNQ

Kothrud
☕☕☕☕☕☕

Seating: Located just opposite of Karishma Society, this cafe has outdoor seating just at the street level which can be noisy because of the traffic right outside. The indoor seating upstairs has a nice long high-table perfect for using laptops, complete with charging ports and overhead lights. Beverages: A solid variety of coffee based drinks. Food: Some of the best preparations of sandwiches I've had in Pune. Atmosphere: Depending on the time, upstairs can be either perfectly pleasant or too noisy because of the crowd. People tend to talk loudly here and you'll often encounter families or large groups here in the evenings. Music selection is a mix of pop and niche indie music (if I'm guessing this right). Volume is never too loud. Pricing: Parking: You'll probably find space for two-wheelers nearby and they have valet for cars. The loo: A single clean unisex restroom that has been smelly on occassions.

Try this: Thecha sandwiches
Visited Aug 2026

Bombon

NIBM
☕☕☕

Seating: This place has a spacious outdoors with a proper rain cover. Tables are placed at comfortable distances. They do also have a few tables indoors around the barista counter. Beverages: Besides their Subko range, I found nothing special. Food: Atmosphere: It's spacious Pricing: Many beverages go beyond 400, which is definitely overpriced for what you get. Cappuccino = 240. Parking: Two-wheeler parking can be difficult to find because there are a thousand two-wheelers already parked on the street. You could park in the mall opposite to the cafe if needed. They have valet for cars. The loo: Separate men's and women's restrooms.

Order this: Cortado + cardamom bun
Visited August 2026

Green Valley
☕☕☕☕☕

Plant-filled, quiet, plenty of outlets — my go-to for working remotely for a few hours. Wifi is solid and nobody rushes you off a table.
